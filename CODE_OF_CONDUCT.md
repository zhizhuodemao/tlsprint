# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

tlsprint is a **TLS-protocol research** project. It is intended to help people
understand, test, and defend against browser-fingerprint-based anti-bot
systems. We expect every contributor and user to use it **ethically and
legally**.

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Intended use

- Only test against systems **you own or are explicitly authorized to test**.
- Respect the Terms of Service of any site you interact with.
- Use the fingerprints and transport capabilities for research, education,
  and defensive/security testing — **not** to circumvent protections you are
  not authorized to bypass, and not to cause harm.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using respectful and inclusive language.
- Being welcoming to new contributors.
- Focusing on technical merit and constructive feedback.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Promoting misuse of the library against systems you don't have permission
  to test.
- Harassment, public or private; trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Unlawful activity in any form.

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and to ban temporarily or permanently any
contributor for other behaviours that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful. We will address any reported abuse privately, timely,
and favourably.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
is representing the project or its community in public spaces.
